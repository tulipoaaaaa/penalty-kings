# Legal notes (round 6 F31): not legal advice

These are risk notes for the builder and reviewers, **not legal advice**. A qualified lawyer must review the game before any live promotion.

- **Live paid chance with a redeemable token prize resembles gambling** in many places. You pay RF for a ball whose value is decided by chance, and you can cash it back into RF. Many jurisdictions regulate this, as do some app stores and payment providers.
- **The judged preview is fully simulated.** No real RF, prizes or transactions, and every economic figure is labelled SIMULATED.
- **Before any live stadium is promoted:**
  - an **18+ gate** (age confirmation) before the first purchase;
  - a **restricted-regions notice**, e.g. Belgium and the Netherlands (loot-box rules) and any region where paid chance games are prohibited, with play blocked where required;
  - **responsible-play** copy and a link (spend limits, cool-off, support resources);
  - **odds shown before every purchase** (already in the game: the odds table on every pack, and the 90% average return);
  - **no "you will win" language** (already enforced by copy rules and tests).
- **Tokens:** $GBOOT has no promised value or returns. The docs and UI never describe it as an investment. Lacing gives cosmetic, XP and seeding perks only, with no yield (round 6 F26).
- **Payments on-ramp (the v1.1 app):** MoonPay and similar providers do their own KYC; the app must not route prohibited regions to them.
- **Official Rare Friends publication** needs Rare Friends' own review.

The checklist item "legal review before live promotion" is in `docs/HUMAN-CHECKS.md`.
